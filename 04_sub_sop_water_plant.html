SOP 05: WATER TREATMENT PLANT OPERATIONS

1. Mandatory PPE Required

  • Chemical safety goggles / full face shield during dosing chemical mixing.
  • Heavy-duty nitrile or PVC chemical apron and elbow-length gloves.
  • Rubber safety boots with steel toes.
  • Vapor respirator mask when handling chlorine or flocculant powders.

2. Daily Routine & Water Quality Monitoring

  • Daily Logging: Record free chlorine levels, pH, and turbidity daily in the Water Plant Log Register.
  • Target Operating Thresholds: Free Chlorine: 0.5 – 1.5 mg/L | pH: 6.5 – 8.5 | Turbidity: < 1.0 NTU.
  • Lab Sampling: Collect monthly microbial samples for accredited third-party laboratory analysis (SANS 241 compliance).

3. Plant Safety & Maintenance Rules

  • Backwash Protocol: Execute sand filter backwash cycles when pressure differential gauges exceed 50 kPa above clean baseline.
  • Chemical Storage: Store chlorine and coagulant chemicals in a cool, dry, ventilated chemical store room. Never store acid and chlorine together.
  • Dam Perimeter Safety: Ensure dam intake perimeter fencing, warning signs, and lifebuoy rings are intact and accessible.
