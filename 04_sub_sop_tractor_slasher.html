SOP 01: MASSEY FERGUSON 268 XTRA TRACTOR & SLASHER OPERATION

1. Mandatory PPE Required

  • Steel-toe safety boots (SANS approved).
  • Hearing protection (Earmuffs or earplugs, Class 4 minimum).
  • High-visibility vest.
  • Safety glasses / eye protection against flying debris.

2. Pre-Operation Checks

  • Inspect fuel, engine oil, hydraulic oil, and coolant levels on the MF268 Xtra.
  • Ensure the PTO master shield and slasher rubber/chain guards are fully intact and spin freely.
  • Verify brake pedals are latched together for road travel.
  • Inspect tyre pressure (Front: 28–32 PSI | Rear: 16–20 PSI) and check wheel nut tightness.
  • Confirm trailing casters and skid runners hold slasher deck 2–3 cm clear of the ground.

3. Operational Safety Rules

  • Gear Selection: Always select Low Range gears (L1–L3) during slashing or slope work. Do not ride the clutch pedal.
  • No Passengers: Never allow extra riders on the tractor or trailing equipment.
  • Exclusion Zone: Maintain a 50-meter clear radius around the slasher during operation to protect residents and bystanders from flying debris.
  • Slope Rules: Drive straight up and down steep inclines—never traverse high slopes laterally to prevent rollover.
  • Lockout Rule: Turn off engine, remove ignition key, and wait for all rotating parts to stop completely before clearing slasher blockages or conducting maintenance.
