SOP 04: CHAINSAW OPERATIONS & ALIEN CLEARING

1. Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Chainsaw safety chaps or cut-retardant trousers (Class 1 / 20 m/s rating).
  • Chainsaw safety helmet with mesh face shield and integrated ear defenders (SNR 28+).
  • Steel-toe safety boots with high-grip soles.
  • Heavy-duty leather gloves (left-hand cut-resistant preferred).

2. Pre-Start Safety Checks

  • Check chain tension, bar oil level, engine fuel mix, and chain sharp condition.
  • Chain Brake Check: Engage chain brake manually and test inertia brake function before cutting.
  • Ensure all anti-vibration mounts and chain catchers are intact and secure.

3. Operational & Felling Protocols

  • Two-Man Rule: Chainsaw operations must strictly never be performed alone. A secondary spotter must be present outside the drop zone.
  • Exclusion Zone: Establish a clear exclusion radius equal to twice (2x) the height of the targeted tree during felling operations.
  • Escape Route: Plan and clear two escape routes at 45-degree angles opposite the intended fall direction before initiating the face notch cut.
  • Kickback Prevention: Never cut with the nose/tip of the guide bar. Hold saw firmly with both hands, thumb wrapped around front handlebar.
  • Transit Rule: Engage chain brake and turn off engine when moving between cutting positions or walking further than 3 meters.
