SOP 01: CONTROLLED BURNING & VELD FIREBREAK PROTOCOLS

CRITICAL RISK CATEGORY (Score 25): Runaway veld fires present extreme hazard to life and property. Strict radio verification and authorization protocols must be followed at all times.

1. Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Flame-retardant legal overalls (SANS approved / Proban or Nomex).
  • Leather fire-fighting safety boots with heat-resistant soles.
  • Heavy-duty leather fire gloves.
  • Safety goggles (non-vented against smoke and hot embers) and dust mask/respirator.
  • Hard hat or fire helmet with neck protector flap.

2. Pre-Burn & Authorization Requirements

  • FDI Check: Check the daily Fire Danger Index (FDI). Burning is strictly forbidden if FDI is Yellow, Orange, or Red.
  • Fire Association Notification: Verify local FPA (Fire Protection Association) permit and notify adjacent landowners at least 24–48 hours prior.
  • Mandatory Radio Check: Perform a strict 2-way radio operational check across all crew members before lighting. The Estate Manager must issue final verbal clearance over radio.
  • Equipment Readiness: Ensure fire tender unit (bakkie-sakkie) is filled, tested, and positioned at the burner line with beaters and knapsack sprayers ready.

3. Operational Burning Rules

  • Burn Direction: Always ignite back-burns (against prevailing wind direction) first to create a secure black-line buffer.
  • Communication Protocol: Maintain constant radio communication between the lead burner, flankers, and the fire tender driver.
  • Immediate Abort Rule: If wind speed exceeds 15 km/h, switches direction unexpectedly, or humidity drops below 30%, extinguish lighting torches immediately and mop up.
  • Mop-Up & Post-Burn Patrol: Cold-trail all burnt edges. Douse all glowing stumps and smoldering cow dung. Maintain an active watch patrol for a minimum of 2 hours post-extinction.
