LEGAL NOTICE: Section 37(1) of the OHS Act holds the Mandator (HOA) criminally liable for offenses committed by contractors on site unless an agreement is formally executed under Section 37(2). By signing this agreement, the Mandatary assumes full operational and legal responsibility for OHS compliance on site.

1. Statutory Acknowledgment & Transfer of Mandate

1.1. The Mandatary acknowledges that it is an employer in its own right under Section 1 of the Occupational Health and Safety Act (Act 85 of 1993, as amended) and agrees to fully comply with all provisions of the Act, the Construction Regulations (2014), and all other applicable regulations.

1.2. In accordance with Section 37(2) of the OHS Act, the Mandatary hereby agrees that the Mandator (Cathkin Estates HOA) shall not be held liable or accountable for any offenses, accidents, injuries, or non-compliance committed by the Mandatary, its employees, agents, or sub-contractors on estate property.

2. COIDA & Compensation Commissioner Compliance

2.1. The Mandatary warrants that it is fully registered and in good standing with the Compensation Commissioner under the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act (COIDA Act 130 of 1993).

2.2. A valid, current Letter of Good Standing from the Compensation Commissioner or an authorized COIDA insurer must be submitted to the Estate Manager prior to site access and must remain valid for the duration of the works.

3. Integration with Estate Rules & Builder Regulations

3.1. This agreement explicitly incorporates and binds the Mandatary to the terms set out in the following Cathkin Estates governance documents:

  • Appendix C1 Part 5: Cathkin Estates Conduct Rules for Builders (covering environmental controls, noise, hours of work, access control, and vehicle limits).
  • Appendix C1 Part 7 (Certificate 2): Builder's Acceptance of Conduct Rules.
  • Estate OHS Baseline Framework: Applicable Safe Operating Procedures (SOPs) for high-risk work.

3.2. A signed copy of Certificate 2 must be executed concurrently with this agreement.

4. Mandatory Operational Safety & Risk Requirements

The Mandatary agrees to enforce the following mandatory controls while operating within Cathkin Estates:

  1. Risk Assessments & Method Statements: A written Baseline Risk Assessment and Task-Specific Method Statement must be conducted and filed on site before work commences.
  2. Supervision & Competency: In terms of Construction Regulation 8, a competent, full-time Site Supervisor must be appointed in writing and be present on site during working hours.
  3. Public Liability Insurance: Maintain active Public Liability Cover for a minimum of R1,000,000 (One Million Rand) specifically covering damage to estate infrastructure, roads, or private properties.
  4. Vehicle & Traffic Restrictions: Strict adherence to the 40 km/h speed limit and the 10,000 kg Gross Vehicle Mass (GVM) weight limit. Ready-mix concrete trucks and dual-rear axle heavy vehicles are strictly prohibited without prior written Estate Manager approval.
  5. Fire Prevention Protocol: Absolute prohibition of open fires anywhere on site or estate property. Hot work (welding/cutting) requires an authorized Hot Work Permit and dedicated 9kg DCP fire extinguishers on site.
  6. Environmental Controls: Full compliance with site containment, mandatory chemical toilet provision (serviced weekly), 3m x 3m waste receptacles, and strict prevention of runoff into estate natural watercourses.

5. Incident Reporting & Right of Audit / Site Shutdown

5.1. The Mandatary shall immediately report any Section 24 reportable incident, serious injury, or environmental spill to the Cathkin Estates Manager within 2 hours of occurrence.

5.2. Cathkin Estates HOA reserves the right to conduct unannounced safety audits of the construction site. Any breach of safety, failure of PPE, expired COIDA standing, or disregard of estate rules will result in an immediate site shutdown and access revocation, without compensation for delay, as provided under Rule 12.2 and 17.3 of the Builders' Conduct Rules.

6. Duration & Scope of Mandate

This agreement shall remain in full force and effect for the entire duration of the Mandatary's presence or building operations within Cathkin Estates, including contractual snagging and site de-establishment.
