1. Executive Summary & Legal Framework

Under Section 16(1) of the Occupational Health and Safety Act (Act 85 of 1993), ultimate legal liability for health, safety, and workplace environmental compliance rests with the Chief Executive Officer or Board Chairman of Cathkin Estates HOA.

To ensure effective operational management across estate maintenance, contractor activities, and common property, Section 16(2) provides the statutory mechanism to delegate specific health and safety duties to competent personnel (such as the Estate Manager or designated Operations Lead).

HSSE GOVERNANCE & IMPLEMENTATION MODEL
Cathkin Estates HOA maintains governance oversight through specialized committee structures:
  • HSSE Committee: Safety policy, environmental compliance, risk registers, and audits.
  • Operations Committee: Estate infrastructure, equipment maintenance, and project execution.
  • Finance Committee: Budgetary allocation, repair limits, and fiscal controls.
  • Architectural Review Committee (ARC): Architectural compliance, building guidelines, and contractor construction controls.

5. OHS Governance & Committee Framework

Strategic oversight is maintained across the specialized committees (HSSE, Operations, Finance, and ARC), with Estate Management executing safety and environmental protocols on the ground.

HSSE & ARC Committee Collaboration:

  • HSSE Committee: Audits safety compliance, chemical storage, hazard registers, and COIDA standings.
  • Architectural Review Committee (ARC): Oversees building site environmental management plans (EMPs), contractor compliance with estate building rules, and construction site safety protocols.

Operations Implementation Responsibilities:

  • Daily on-the-ground implementation of safety checklists (vehicles, tractors, chemical store).
  • Enforcing PPE compliance, low-range gear selection on machinery, and operational safety rules.
  • Conducting daily toolbox talks, hazard identification, and contractor site inductions.
  • Immediate reporting of incidents and operational hazards to the HSSE Committee.
