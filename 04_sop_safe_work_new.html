1. Purpose & Administrative Scope

Safe Operating Procedures (SOPs) define mandatory operational steps to protect estate staff, contractors, and residents from harm. In accordance with Section 8(2)(e) of the OHS Act, all operators must be formally trained and declared competent on these SOPs before being authorized to execute high-risk maintenance tasks.
