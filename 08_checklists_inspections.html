MODULE 08: OPERATIONAL INSPECTIONS & CHECKLISTS

Operational Health & Safety Governance Manual | Cathkin Estates HOA
Document Ref: OHS-MOD-007 Regulatory Standard: OHS Act General Machinery & Driven Machinery Regulations Digitization: Paper Forms & Airtable Integration

1. Executive Summary

Routine pre-start and periodic inspections prevent equipment failure, ensure worker safety, and maintain statutory compliance across estate operations. All pre-start checklists are logged physically on site by operators and reviewed by management or submitted directly into the estate's Airtable Operational Management Base[cite: 4].
