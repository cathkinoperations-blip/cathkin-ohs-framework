Cathkin Estates HOA — High-Risk Task Baseline Assessment

Task Focus: Firebreaks & Controlled Burning Operations [1] Baseline Risk Score: 25 (Critical)

Task Sequence Potential Hazard Risk Level Mandatory Controls & Safety Standard
1. Pre-Burn Planning & Setup Unfavorable wind conditions, lack of emergency contacts, untrained staff. High Verify burning schedule (Doc D2)[1]. Print/post emergency contact list (Okhahlamba Fire Dept, neighbours)[1]. Check FDI (Fire Danger Index) prior to ignition.
2. Controlled Firebreak Burning Runaway veld fire, burn injuries, smoke inhalation, crew entrapment. Critical Full PPE mandatory (leather gloves, flame-retardant overalls, face shields). Dedicated fire unit on standby. Maintain clear escape routes and team radio communications.
3. Mop-Up & Post-Burn Inspection Smoldering embers re-igniting after crew leaves area. High Douse all hot spots and stumps with water. Conduct minimum 1-hour patrol post-extinction before declaring area safe.

Task Focus: High Voltage (11kV) & Electrical Substation Maintenance [1] Baseline Risk Score: 25 (Critical)

Task Sequence Potential Hazard Risk Level Mandatory Controls & Safety Standard
1. Substation Inspection & Liaison Arc flash, accidental contact with live high-voltage components. Critical Eskom liaison protocol must be followed (Doc D5)[1]. Only certified/registered electricians permitted to access live HT panels or transformers[1].
2. Isolation & Lockout (LOTO) Unexpected energization while maintenance is underway. Critical Strict Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedure required. Test for dead before starting work. Notify affected residents in advance of planned outages[1].

Task Focus: Tractor, Slasher & Heavy Equipment Operation Baseline Risk Score: 20 (Critical)

Task Sequence Potential Hazard Risk Level Mandatory Controls & Safety Standard
1. Pre-Start Inspection Fluid leaks, faulty brakes, damaged PTO guards, missing ROPS. High Complete daily pre-start logbook. Do not operate equipment if PTO guard or brakes are compromised.
2. Equipment Hitching Crush injuries between tractor and trailing slasher. Critical Ensure second person stands clear during reversing. Lock pin with safety linchpin before connecting PTO drive.
3. Field Mowing & Slashing Rollover on slopes, flying stones/debris hitting bystanders. Critical No mowing on gradients exceeding safety limit. Maintain 50-meter clear exclusion zone from residents, vehicles, and livestock. Safety glasses and hearing protection mandatory.
4. Maintenance & Debris Clearing Accidental engagement of PTO while clearing jammed slasher. Critical Turn off tractor engine, remove key from ignition, and wait for all rotating parts to stop completely before approaching slasher blades (Lockout/Tagout principle).

Task Focus: Chainsaw Operation & Tree Felling / Alien Clearing Baseline Risk Score: 20 (Critical)

Task Sequence Potential Hazard Risk Level Mandatory Controls & Safety Standard
1. Chainsaw Pre-Use Checks Chain derailment, chain brake failure, fuel leaks. High Test chain brake, chain tension, and throttle latch. Fuel unit outdoors with engine off and cool.
2. Tree Felling & Bush Cutting Kickback, cut/laceration injuries from blade, falling timber onto operators. Critical Only certified operators permitted. Chainsaw chaps/cut-resistant trousers, face shield, hard hat, and steel-toe boots mandatory. Maintain 2x tree height exclusion zone.
3. Branch Snagging & Stacking Eye injuries, pinch injuries, trip hazards on uneven ground. Medium Wear leather work gloves and safety glasses. Keep work areas clear of timber clutter.

Task Focus: Water Treatment Plant Operation & Chemical Dosing [1] Baseline Risk Score: 15 (High)

Task Sequence Potential Hazard Risk Level Mandatory Controls & Safety Standard
1. Chemical Dosing & Mixing Corrosive chemical splashes (chlorine/coagulants), toxic fumes. High Trained operators only (Estate Manager + designated staff, Doc D4)[1]. Wear chemical apron, rubber gloves, and eye/face protection. Ensure eyewash bottle is filled.
2. Sampling & Quality Inspection Pathogen exposure, slipping near water storage/dam. Medium Monthly independent lab testing program[1]. Ranger field leak inspections[1]. Slip-resistant footwear required around wet treatment pads.

Task Focus: Herbicide & Hazardous Chemical Application Baseline Risk Score: 12 (High)

Task Sequence Potential Hazard Risk Level Mandatory Controls & Safety Standard
1. Decanting & Mixing Chemical splash to eyes/skin, toxic vapor inhalation. High Decant in designated bunded, ventilated store. Wear face shield, chemical apron, nitrile gloves, and PVC boots. Keep eyewash station accessible.
2. Field Application Spray drift onto non-target areas, operator exposure, applicator leakage. Medium Do not spray in wind speeds exceeding 15 km/h. Inspect knapsack sprayers for leaks prior to filling. Wash hands before eating/drinking.
