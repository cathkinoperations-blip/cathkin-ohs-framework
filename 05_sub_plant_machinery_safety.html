MODULE 05: PLANT, MACHINERY & VEHICLES

Operational Health & Safety Governance Manual | Cathkin Estates HOA
Document Ref: OHS-MOD-005 Regulatory Standard: DMR 2 & GMR 2 (OHS Act §8) Target Group: Operations Supervisor, Tractor Operators & Maintenance Crew

1. Legislative Mandate & Operational Scope

In accordance with the Driven Machinery Regulations (DMR 2) and General Machinery Regulations (GMR 2) framed under the Occupational Health and Safety Act, Cathkin Estates HOA enforces stringent safety measures for all estate-owned and operated plant, agricultural machinery, and utility vehicles. Given the estate's remote topography and extensive grounds maintenance requirements, strict adherence to pre-start checks and safe operating procedures is mandatory.

2. Massey Ferguson 268 Xtra Tractor & Slasher Safety Controls

The estate utilizes a Massey Ferguson 268 Xtra tractor and associated towed slasher for heavy-duty brush clearing, firebreak maintenance, and road reserve upkeep. Operating agricultural tractors presents high-risk rollover, entanglement, and strike hazards.

Machine Component Mandatory Safety & Compliance Requirement Frequency Responsible Party
ROPS & Seatbelts Ensure the Roll-Over Protective Structure (ROPS) is securely bolted and undamaged. Operators must wear fitted seatbelts at all times while operating on slopes. Daily / Pre-Start Tractor Operator
PTO Shaft & Guards Verify that the Power Take-Off (PTO) master shield and rotating plastic tunnel guards are intact, undamaged, and securely locked in place before engaging the implement. Daily / Pre-Start Tractor Operator
Hydraulic Lines & Brakes Inspect hydraulic hoses for weeping leaks, bulges, or chafing. Test service and parking brakes on level ground before undertaking field deployment. Daily / Pre-Start Tractor Operator
Slasher Deck & Blades Inspect swing blades for cracking, excessive wear, or missing bolts. Ensure safety chains and deflector flaps are fully operational to prevent projectile strikes. Weekly Check Maintenance Crew

3. General Workshop Plant & Power Tools

Stationary and portable power tools located in the estate workshop—such as bench grinders, welding units, pillar drills, and air compressors—must be operated only by authorized, competent personnel.

Key Workshop Safety Rules
  • Bench Grinders: Eye shields must be adjusted correctly, and the work rest gap must not exceed 3mm from the grinding wheel face.
  • Electrical Safety: All portable tools must be inspected monthly and verified against the workshop Residual Current Circuit Breaker (RCCD) earth-leakage test log.
  • Lock-Out / Tag-Out (LOTO): Any machine undergoing blade changes, belt adjustments, or repairs must be completely isolated, locked out, and tagged with an "Out of Service" warning notice.
