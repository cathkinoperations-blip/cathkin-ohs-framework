MODULE 05: GENERAL WORKPLACE SAFETY & HOUSEKEEPING

Operational Health & Safety Governance Manual | Cathkin Estates HOA
Document Ref: OHS-MOD-005 Regulatory Standards: Environmental Regulations for Workplaces & Facilities Regulations Target Group: Operations Supervisor, Workshop & Maintenance Crew

1. Legislative Mandate & Scope

In accordance with the Environmental Regulations for Workplaces and Facilities Regulations framed under the Occupational Health and Safety Act, Cathkin Estates HOA is committed to maintaining a safe, structured, and hygienic work environment. This module sets mandatory standards for maintenance workshops, storage yards, field operations, and personal protective equipment (PPE) governance.

2. General Housekeeping & Workplace Standards (5S Principles)

All estate operational zones—including the main maintenance workshop, tractor bays, chemical stores, and waste sites—must maintain strict housekeeping standards to mitigate slip, trip, fall, and fire hazards.

Area / Focus Zone Mandatory Housekeeping & Safety Requirement Frequency Responsible Party
Workshop Walkways & Floors Keep marked gangways clear of tools, oil spills, and cables. Clean oil leaks immediately using spill absorbent granules. Daily Workshop Staff
Illumination & Lighting Ensure a minimum of 150 lux in storage areas and 300 lux over workshop workbenches and repair bays. Replace flickering or broken bulbs immediately. Monthly Audit Operations Supervisor
Ventilation & Air Quality Ensure mechanical and cross-ventilation in chemical rooms and welding areas to prevent dust, fume, and vapour accumulation. Continuous All Maintenance Staff
Ergonomics & Heavy Lifting Enforce safe lifting limits (max 25kg individual lift). Utilize team lifts or mechanical hoists/cranes for heavier tractor implements and machinery components. Continuous All Operational Staff
Scrap & Waste Disposal Separate scrap metal, oily rags, domestic waste, and hazardous waste into designated, labeled, fire-safe bins. Empty hazardous waste bins before overflow. Weekly Grounds & Maintenance Crew

3. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Matrix & Issuance Protocol

Under OHS Act §8(2)(e), the HOA provides all required safety clothing and equipment free of charge. Employees are legally bound to wear assigned PPE when executing operational duties.

Operational Task Required Safety Gear / PPE Regulatory / SANS Standard
Tractor & Slasher Operation Steel toe cap boots, high-visibility vest, heavy-duty ear muffs (SNR ≥28dB), eye protection goggles. SANS 20345 / SANS 1451
Chainsaw & Alien Vegetation Clearing Chainsaw protective chaps/leggings, hard hat with face shield, ear defenders, cut-resistant leather gloves, steel toe boots. SANS 1397 / ISO 11393
Herbicides & Chemical Handling Nitrile long-cuff gloves, chemical splash apron, face shield/goggles, half-mask respirator with ABEK1 filter, PVC boots. SANS 10234 / EN 140
Workshop Grinding & Welding Welding helmet / grinding face shield, leather welding spats, heavy leather apron, flame-retardant overall, ear plugs. SANS 471 / SANS 1435
General Grounds Maintenance Standard overall suit, steel toe boots, high-visibility vest, heavy-duty cloth gloves, sun hat/caps. SANS 434
