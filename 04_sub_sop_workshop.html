SOP 08: WORKSHOP SAFE WORK & POWER TOOLS

1. Mandatory Workshop PPE

  • Impact-resistant safety glasses (SANS 1400 / ANSI Z87.1 certified).
  • Steel-toe safety boots.
  • Hearing protection (earplugs/earmuffs) when using grinders, drills, or saws.
  • Leather welding gloves and auto-darkening shield during arc welding/grinding.

2. Power Tool Safety & Guarding Rules

  • Grinder Safety: Never operate an angle grinder or bench grinder without its safety guard in place. Disc RPM rating must equal or exceed tool maximum RPM.
  • Electrical Safety: Connect all 230V portable power tools through an operational Earth Leakage (RCCD) circuit breaker. Inspect cables for exposed wires before plug-in.
  • Tool Inspections: Conduct monthly visual and operational tool audits; tag out damaged or malfunctioning tools immediately.

3. Workshop Housekeeping & Hot Work

  • Hot Work Clearance: Clear flammable materials (gas cans, oily rags, dry grass) within 5 meters prior to grinding or welding. Position a 9kg DCP fire extinguisher adjacent to the work area.
  • General Housekeeping: Keep workshop floors clean, dry, and clear of grease, oil drips, and tripping hazards. Store gas cylinders upright and secured with safety chains.
