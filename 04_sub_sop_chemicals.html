SOP 06: HAZARDOUS CHEMICAL HANDLING & HERBICIDES

1. Mandatory PPE Required

  • Nitrile or neoprene chemical-resistant gloves (long cuff).
  • Safety goggles or full face protection shield.
  • Impermeable chemical apron or Tyvek suit during concentrate mixing.
  • Half-mask respirator fitted with organic vapor / particulate cartridges (A2P3).

2. Storage & Bunding Standards

  • SDS Availability: Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for all stored chemicals must be posted outside the chemical store room.
  • Bund Compliance: Storage facility must feature a 110% capacity containment bund to capture liquid spills.
  • Access Control: Chemical store must remain locked with access restricted strictly to trained, authorized operators.

3. Application & Environmental Safety Rules

  • Aquatic Buffer Zones: Maintain a mandatory 15-meter buffer zone around streams, dams, and wetland channels when applying non-aquatic rated herbicides.
  • Weather Restrictions: Do not spray herbicides if wind speed exceeds 10 km/h or rain is forecasted within 4 hours.
  • Spill Response: In the event of a chemical spill, apply absorbent spill kit material immediately, contain run-off, dispose of contaminated material as hazardous waste, and notify the Estate Manager.
