Cathkin Estates HOA — Master Baseline Risk Matrix & Register

1. 5x5 Risk Evaluation Matrix

Risk Score = Likelihood (1-5) x Severity (1-5)

Severity →
Likelihood ↓		 1 - Insignificant 2 - Minor 3 - Moderate 4 - Major 5 - Catastrophic
5 - Almost Certain 5 (Med) 10 (High) 15 (High) 20 (Critical) 25 (Critical)
4 - Likely 4 (Low) 8 (Med) 12 (High) 16 (Critical) 20 (Critical)
3 - Possible 3 (Low) 6 (Med) 9 (Med) 12 (High) 15 (High)
2 - Unlikely 2 (Low) 4 (Low) 6 (Med) 8 (Med) 10 (High)
1 - Rare 1 (Low) 2 (Low) 3 (Low) 4 (Low) 5 (Med)

2. Estate Baseline Risk Register

Activity / Area Hazard Identified Inherent Risk Score Mandatory Risk Controls Residual Risk
Controlled Burning & Firebreaks Runaway veld fires, smoke inhalation, severe burns, entrapment. 25 (Critical) Controlled burning program (Doc D2)[1], emergency contact list posted/printed (Okhahlamba Emergency, neighbours)[1], mandatory PPE (leather gloves, fire retardant suits), trained fire-fighting staff[1]. 8 (Med)
High Voltage (11kV) & LV Transformer Maintenance Electrocution, arc flash, transformer oil leaks, total power outage. 25 (Critical) Eskom liaison protocol[1], strictly licensed/contracted electricians for repairs[1], lockout/tagout (LOTO), perimeter fencing on substations/transformers, prompt homeowner notifications[1]. 5 (Med)
Tractor & Slasher Operations PTO entanglement, rollover on steep slopes, flying debris. 20 (Critical) Mandatory PTO master guard, ROPS frame, pre-start checks, 50m exclusion zone for bystander residents. 6 (Med)
Chainsaw Operations & Alien Clearing Severe lacerations, kickback, falling timber, high noise/vibration exposure. 20 (Critical) Chainsaw chaps/leggings, face shield, earmuffs, pre-use chain brake checks, certified/trained operators, drop zone exclusion area. 6 (Med)
Water Treatment Plant Operation Chemical dosing exposure, waterborne pathogen contamination, pipe leaks, drowning risk at dam. 15 (High) Operation training for Estate Manager + 2 staff (Doc D4)[1], monthly professional lab water testing[1], field ranger leak inspections[1], chemical PPE, perimeter security at dam. 4 (Low)
Chemical Handling (Herbicides) Skin absorption, eye injury, environmental contamination / spill. 12 (High) SDS available at store, 110% bunded storage, chemical aprons, face shields, nitrile gloves, spill kit on vehicle. 4 (Low)
Perimeter & Game Fence Maintenance Electric shock from game fence, wild animal escapes/attacks, security breaches. 12 (High) Monthly security equipment checks (Doc D3/D6)[1], warning signage on electric fence, ranger patrols, wild animal handling procedures. 4 (Low)
Road Repairs & Asphalt Work Traffic exposure, burn hazards, dust inhalation, manual lifting. 10 (High) High-visibility vests, traffic cones, warning signage, dust masks, thermal gloves for hot-mix asphalt handling. 3 (Low)
Workshop Maintenance Power tool injuries, eye hazards, electrical shock. 9 (Med) Monthly power tool inspection, RCCD protection, safety glasses/goggles mandatory. 2 (Low)
Wildlife Handling & Game Counts Animal bites/trampling, vehicle collisions during counts or drives. 9 (Med) Twice-yearly structured game counts (Doc D1-1)[1], board approval before animal addition/removal[1], distance maintenance guidelines, safe vehicle operation. 3 (Low)
