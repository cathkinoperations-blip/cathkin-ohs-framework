MODULE 01: LEGAL FRAMEWORK & STATUTORY APPOINTMENTS

Operational Health & Safety Governance Manual | Cathkin Estates HOA
Document Ref: OHS-MOD-001 Regulatory Standard: OHS Act (Act 85 of 1993) §16.1 & §16.2 Target Group: HOA Board, Operations & Finance Committee

1. Executive Summary & Legal Framework

Under Section 16(1) of the Occupational Health and Safety Act (Act 85 of 1993), ultimate legal liability for health, safety, and workplace environmental compliance rests with the Chief Executive Officer or Board Chairman of Cathkin Estates HOA.

To ensure effective operational management across estate maintenance, contractor activities, and common property, Section 16(2) provides the statutory mechanism to delegate specific health and safety duties to competent personnel (such as the Estate Manager or designated Committee Lead).

STATUTORY PRINCIPLE
A Section 16(2) appointment delegates operational responsibility and legal authority, but under South African law, the Section 16(1) officer retains ultimate governance accountability oversight.

4. OHS Committee Governance & Meeting Cadence

Under Section 19 of the OHS Act, health and safety oversight is integrated into the monthly Operations Committee agenda.

Standing Agenda Items:

  • Review of previous month's incident/near-miss log (GAR Annexure 1).
  • Status of contractor Section 37(2) agreements & COIDA Letters of Good Standing.
  • Equipment and machinery safety inspection reports (Tractor, slashers, power tools).
  • Hazardous Chemical Substance (HCS) bunding and spill kit verification.
  • Review of open Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) items.
