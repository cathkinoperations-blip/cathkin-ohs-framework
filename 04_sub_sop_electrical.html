SOP 02: ELECTRICAL INFRASTRUCTURE & LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO)

CRITICAL RISK CATEGORY (Score 25): High voltage (11kV) transformers and distribution boards present lethal electrocution hazards. Unqualified estate staff are strictly prohibited from opening live high-voltage enclosures.

1. Scope & Authorized Access

  • Eskom / High Voltage Limit: Only certified, registered high-voltage electricians or authorized Eskom personnel may open, work on, or manipulate 11kV infrastructure.
  • Estate Staff Duties: Estate staff are restricted to external perimeter audits, padlock integrity verification, landscape clearing around enclosures, and Low Voltage (LV) maintenance under LOTO.

2. Enclosure Security & Physical Safeguards

  • Padlock Compliance: All 11kV step-down transformers, miniature substations, and kiosk boards must remain padlocked 24/7.
  • Monthly Audit: Conduct a monthly visual perimeter audit to check for open cabinet doors, damaged locks, broken warning signs, or active oil leaks.
  • Clearance Zone: Maintain a strict 2-meter vegetation-free clear zone around all electrical kiosks and transformers to prevent fire ignition and allow immediate access.

3. Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Execution Rules (LV Systems)

  • Isolate: Switch off the main breaker switch controlling the target pump room or circuit breaker.
  • Lock & Tag: Apply personal red padlock and danger tag ("DANGER DO NOT OPERATE") to the isolator switch handle. Only the person who attached the lock holds the key.
  • Zero Energy Verification: Test circuit with a calibrated multi-meter/voltage tester to verify zero electrical current prior to starting any maintenance.
