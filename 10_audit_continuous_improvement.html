1. Audit Governance & Compliance Lifecycle

To ensure continuous statutory compliance and proactive risk mitigation, Cathkin Estates implements a structured three-tier auditing framework. This framework bridges daily operational checks with rigorous annual management reviews to ensure all findings are tracked to resolution using Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) logs.

Audit Tier Frequency Scope & Focus Areas Responsible Party
Tier 1: Operational Checklists Daily / Weekly Pre-start tractor checks, equipment safety guards, workplace housekeeping, and portable tool inspections. Foreman / Maintenance Crew
Tier 2: Committee Reviews Monthly Review of incident registers, hazard logs, first aid box audits, and contractor safety files. Operations Committee
Tier 3: Annual Statutory Audit Annually Comprehensive evaluation across all core compliance pillars, statutory legal appointments, and policy updates. Independent Auditor / 16(2) Appointee

2. Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) Workflow

Whenever an audit discrepancy, non-conformance, or near-miss is identified, it must be formally recorded on the estate's CAPA log. The workflow ensures accountability and timely closure:

  • Identification & Logging: Record the specific finding, exact location, risk severity level, and responsible owner.
  • Root Cause Analysis: Determine why the failure occurred (e.g., lack of training, equipment breakdown, procedural gap).
  • Corrective Action Implementation: Execute immediate risk reduction measures and assign a target resolution date.
  • Verification & Sign-off: The Operations Supervisor or Safety Appointee verifies the fix before closing the log entry.
