MODULE 02: HAZARD IDENTIFICATION & RISK ASSESSMENT (HIRA)

Operational Health & Safety Governance Manual | Cathkin Estates HOA
Document Ref: OHS-MOD-002 Regulatory Standard: OHS Act §8(2)(d) & General Risk Regulations Target Group: Operations Committee, Estate Manager, Maintenance Team

1. Executive Summary & Statutory Duty

In terms of Section 8(2)(d) of the Occupational Health and Safety Act (Act 85 of 1993), Cathkin Estates HOA is statutorily required to establish the hazards attached to any work performed, any operation conducted, and any plant or machinery used on the estate. Furthermore, effective control measures must be established and enforced to eliminate or reduce these risks.

HIRA GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE
Risk assessment is not a once-off event. The estate baseline HIRA must be reviewed annually, after any significant incident/near-miss, or whenever new equipment, chemicals, or operational procedures are introduced.

2. Hierarchy of Controls

When mitigating identified operational hazards (e.g., tractor operation, slasher clearing, road maintenance, herbicide handling), safety controls must be implemented according to the legally recognized Hierarchy of Controls:

  1. Elimination: Physically remove the hazard (e.g., automating high-risk tasks or eliminating hazardous manual handling).
  2. Substitution: Replace the hazard with a safer alternative (e.g., swapping toxic eco-hazardous chemicals with eco-friendly alternatives).
  3. Engineering Controls: Isolate people from the hazard (e.g., installing PTO guards/shrouds on slashers, ROPS frames on tractors, secondary chemical bunds).
  4. Administrative Controls: Change the way people work (e.g., Safe Operating Procedures, daily pre-start checklists, rotating shift work).
  5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Protect the worker with personal gear (e.g., steel-cap boots, face shields, hearing protection, dust masks). *PPE is always the last line of defense.*

4. Continuous Risk Monitoring

Operational risk reviews are conducted through a 3-tier structure across estate management:

  • Daily: Pre-start inspection checklists (Tractor, power tools, chemical application).
  • Monthly: Operations Lead site inspections and committee review of open risk mitigation tasks.
  • Annually: Complete review of the Baseline Risk Assessment log by the Section 16(2) appointee and HOA Committee.
